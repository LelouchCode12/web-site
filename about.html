Our process

Good short-form video starts before the camera rolls.

This page explains how a mobile production moves from a rough idea to a finished piece. Its timeline layout is intentionally different from the landing and services pages.

01 — Idea

Define the story

We choose the audience, central message, mood and platform before shooting.

02 — Plan

Build the shot list

Angles, movement, locations and transitions are organised into a small plan.

03 — Shoot

Capture with purpose

Every frame has a role: establish, inform, connect or create emotion.

04 — Finish

Edit and export

We shape rhythm, add sound and text, check quality and export for delivery.

Production principles

Three rules guide every scene

Clarity

The viewer should understand what the shot is doing without unnecessary decoration.

Rhythm

Cuts and camera movement support the pace of the story rather than distracting from it.

Consistency

Color, framing, typography and sound create one visual language across the edit.
